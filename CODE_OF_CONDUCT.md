# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project (LR PL-300 Practice Test) exists to help people prepare for the Microsoft PL-300 certification, for free. We want it to be a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone who visits, opens an issue, or contributes, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Giving constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for learners using this practice test
- Showing patience toward newcomers to open source or to the PL-300 material itself

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
- Trolling, or deliberately disruptive or off-topic contributions
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and comments on this repository), and also applies when someone is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the maintainer at **rlashmibai@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and result in a response appropriate to the circumstances. The maintainer is obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter of an incident.

Project maintainers who don't follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions, including removal from the project.
